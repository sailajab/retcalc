Suggested withdrawal order

  1. HSA — for that year's healthcare costs only. Withdrawals for medical expenses are the one way HSA funds come out completely tax-free.
  2. Cash / savings — already-taxed money, spend it first so everything else keeps compounding.
  3. Brokerage (stocks) — taxable, but usually at preferential capital-gains rates.
  4. 401(k) / Traditional IRA — taxed as ordinary income when withdrawn.
  5. Roth IRA — saved for last: it's tax-free, has no required withdrawals, and is the best asset to leave growing (or to leave as a legacy).

Business equity and investment-property equity count toward your net worth but aren't included here — they're treated as illiquid and aren't assumed to fund retirement withdrawals.
